Standard room sizes for Bangladeshi homes (in feet)

By Abdullah Jewel · Updated 2026-10-25

Good room sizes come from the furniture and the movement space around it, not from a rule of the same number for every plot. The ranges below are common starting points for apartments and houses in Bangladesh. Adjust them to your plot and family.

Typical sizes

RoomComfortable size (ft)Notes
Master bedroom12 x 14 to 14 x 16Double bed plus wardrobe and 2 ft of walking space
Child or guest bedroom10 x 12 to 12 x 13Single or double bed, small wardrobe
Living room12 x 16 to 14 x 20Sofa set, TV unit and a clear route to other rooms
Dining area10 x 10 to 10 x 12Four to six seat table with chair pull-out space
Kitchen8 x 10 to 10 x 12Counter, sink, gas point, fridge and ventilation
Attached bathroom5 x 7 to 6 x 8Commode, basin and shower with a dry and wet zone if possible
Common bathroom5 x 6 to 6 x 7Place near the living area
Corridor3 to 3.5 wideTwo people should be able to pass
Balcony4 to 5 deepWide enough for a chair

Clearances that make a room feel right

  • Leave about 2 to 2.5 ft beside and at the foot of a bed.
  • Keep 3 ft between a sofa and a TV unit at the very least, and more for a bigger screen.
  • Door swings should not hit a bed, wardrobe or another door.
  • Every habitable room needs a window to the outside for light and cross ventilation.

Test your plan before building

Drop real furniture into a plan and walk through it. In the PlanCraft 3D planner you can place beds, sofas, counters and sanitary fittings at their real sizes, then switch to first-person view to feel the space.

Sizes are guidance only. Local building rules set minimum room areas and window sizes, so check with your city authority.
