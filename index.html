Questions

Is PlanCraft 3D free?

Yes. You can use the planner in your browser without an account.

Where are my drawings stored?

On your own device. Use Save / Load to keep a copy as a file.

How accurate is the cost estimate?

It is a planning estimate from your drawing and typical Bangladesh rules of thumb. Prices change, so edit the unit rates to your dealer quotes.

Does it replace an engineer?

No. Structural design, soil testing and approvals need a licensed engineer or architect.
